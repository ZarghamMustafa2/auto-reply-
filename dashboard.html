3. Anti-Spam Pacing: Sending bulk broadcasts too fast triggers automated spam blocks. MetaHub enforces configurable pacing delays to keep your Page healthy.

Meta API Configuration Guide

To capture Messenger Page-Scoped User IDs (PSIDs) and broadcast compliant messages, proceed as follows:

  1. Visit the Meta Developers Portal and select Create App.
  2. Assign the **Messenger** product to your newly created application.
  3. Link your designated Facebook Business Page inside the Messenger settings workspace.
  4. Under token generation parameters, extract the unique Page Access Token.
  5. Ensure the **Webhooks** feature is subscribed to the `messages` and `messaging_postbacks` parameters on your backend server.
  6. Copy your credentials into the setting fields above, turn off Demo Mode, and you're ready to communicate officially.
